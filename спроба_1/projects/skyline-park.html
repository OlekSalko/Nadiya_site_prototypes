About the project

Panoramic views over the park and the city skyline.

Skyline Park rises fourteen storeys above Midtown, with a glazed sky lounge on the top floor and apartments oriented toward the park.

A ventilated facade system, triple-glazed windows and individually controlled heating deliver comfort and lower running costs.

The ground floor hosts a concierge lobby, a fitness studio and a co-working area for residents.
